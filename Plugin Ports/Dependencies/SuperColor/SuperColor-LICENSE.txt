Super Color - sample and image asset licence
============================================

SuperColor.flac (Dependencies/SuperColor) holds the samples of unplugred's MPaint plugin
(https://github.com/unplugred/vsts, plugins/mpaint/samples) by unplugred (https://vst.unplug.red): all 15 sounds
with their 21 notes each (MIDI 59-79) joined in order into one stereo file (mono samples copied to both
channels), reduced from 24-bit to 12-bit (stored as 16-bit FLAC).
SuperColorIcons.png and SuperColorError.png are the image assets of the same plugin
(plugins/mpaint/resources: largeicon.png and error.png).

AGPLv3-or-later licence (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html).

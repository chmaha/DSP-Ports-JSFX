Tine Capsule - sample data licence
==================================

TineCapsule.flac (Dependencies/TineCapsule) holds the 65 mono samples of "jRhodes3d" by Jeff Learman
(https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d, a 1977 73-key stage tine piano), requantised from 16 to
14 bits (rounded, no dither), duplicated to two identical channels and joined into one FLAC file, and played by a
JSFX port of the sfizz SFZ engine. TineCapsule.txt holds the region data compiled from the CC0 control files
jRhodes3d-mono.sfz and jRhodes3d-mono-no-xfade.sfz.

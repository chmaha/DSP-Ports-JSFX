Superstitious - sample data notice
==================================

Superstitious.flac (Dependencies/Superstitious) holds 255 clavinet samples, folded to mono (the recordings are
dual-mono) and reduced to 12 bits with dither, joined into one FLAC file. Superstitious.txt holds the region data
compiled from the SFZ mapping "Stevie Clavinet.sfz". Both are played by a JSFX port of the sfizz SFZ engine.

Sources:
- Main samples (60 keys, four velocity layers): an anonymous Clavinet sample set published on musical-artifacts.com
  (https://musical-artifacts.com/artifacts/646, uploaded by "Lithalean"), as cleaned up, extended and mapped by
  Jeff Learman in "Stevie Clavinet" (https://github.com/jlearman/stevie-clavinet).
- Key-release samples: from the Zynthian factory SFZ set (https://github.com/zynthian/zynthian-factory-sfzs,
  sfz/Pianos/Clavinet), as included in Stevie Clavinet.

The original author of these recordings is unknown and no licence was given by any of the sources above. They are
included in good faith as freely circulated samples, with credit to everyone who passed them on. If you are the
rightful owner and want them removed, please open an issue at https://github.com/chmaha/DSP-Ports-JSFX and they
will be removed.

The JSFX code (Superstitious.jsfx) is released separately under the GPLv3+ licence.

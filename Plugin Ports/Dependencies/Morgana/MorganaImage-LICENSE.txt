MorganaImage, MorganaFx, MorganaHead and MorganaSmoke (.png and 2x.png), and MorganaSil.png

Alphonse Mucha (1860-1939), "Clair de lune" (Moonlight), one of the four decorative panels
"La Lune et les étoiles" (The Moon and the Stars), colour lithograph, 1902.

Public domain (the artist died in 1939; the work is out of copyright worldwide where the term is
life + 70 years or less, and in the United States as a pre-1929 publication).

Source scan: Bibliothèque nationale de France, Gallica btv1b90077190, via Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:La_Lune_et_les_%C3%A9toiles_-_panneaux_d%C3%A9coratifs_et_couverture_-_(par)_Mucha_-_btv1b90077190_(4_of_5).jpg
Licence on Commons: Public domain.

The PNGs are a crop of the picture area (the decorative border is not included), resized for the plugin sidebar.
MorganaFx(.png/2x) and MorganaHead(.png/2x) are animation layers derived from the same picture (light sprites,
retouched patches and a head cut-out); they carry the same public-domain status.
MorganaSil.png is a further layer cut from the same picture
(a figure silhouette) and carry the same public-domain status. MorganaSmoke(.png/2x) holds
procedurally generated mist textures made for this plugin; no copyright is claimed in them.

ZynAddSubFX (https://github.com/zynaddsubfx/instruments, banks directory). ZynAddSubFX is
available under the GPL-2.0-or-later license. Each entry names the source file and the author
recorded in it, where there is one.

Only instruments that use the ADsynth engine and no instrument effects were converted.

Pad Thai sound banks

The patches in PadThai.txt were converted from the instrument banks that ship with
ZynAddSubFX (https://github.com/zynaddsubfx/instruments, banks directory). ZynAddSubFX is
available under the GPL-2.0-or-later license. Some contributed banks carry their own terms:
Cris Owl Alvarez (GPL-2.0 / LGPL, see its readme.txt) and The Mysterious Bank
(Creative Commons BY, 2010 My?terious). Each entry names the source file and the author
recorded in it, where there is one.

Only instruments that use the PADsynth engine alone, with no instrument effects, were converted.

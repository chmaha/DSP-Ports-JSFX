Changes made for Rosalina Curiosa: the 24-bit samples were rounded to 16 bits (TPDF dither) and joined into one
FLAC per instrument (stereo samples as one stereo stream; mono samples paired side by side in the two channels);
the SFZ mappings were compiled into the .txt files for the plugins' port of the sfizz engine. The sound is
otherwise unchanged.

Mandochord - sample data licence
================================

Mandochord.flac holds the samples of chmaha's Mandochord SFZ library. They come from PluckedStrings.sf2
(Plucked Strings) by John Maddock (TavyMusic), 2013: "All the instruments were sampled by the author."

Licence: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  "This soundfont and the samples it contains are usable under the Creative Commons CC0 license."
  (PluckedStrings.sf2 info chunk: "Copyright John Maddock 2013, Distributed under the Creative Commons CC0 license.")

Changes made for Mandochord:
  - three of its instruments used: Mando Gibson Oval, Mando Celtic Oval and Dola Celtic Oval (mandola)
  - every sample retuned to A = 440 Hz equal temperament (resampled, no gain change)
  - for this JSFX: the 16-bit samples joined losslessly into one stereo FLAC (two mono lanes)

The mapping (Mandochord.txt, compiled from Mandochord.sfz) is chmaha's own work, released with the JSFX
code under GPLv3+.

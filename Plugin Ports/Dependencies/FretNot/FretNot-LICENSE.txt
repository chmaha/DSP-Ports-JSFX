Fret Not - sample data licence
==============================

The samples and instrument mappings in this folder come from three Karoryfer Samples libraries:

  Emily.flac, Emily.txt - Emilyguitar
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar
    Epiphone Emily the Strange guitar played and mapped by D. Smolken. Copyright 2014 Karoryfer Lecolds.

  Shiny.flac, Shiny.txt - Shinyguitar
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.shinyguitar
    Archtop guitar played and mapped by D. Smolken. Copyright 2015, 2018 Karoryfer Lecolds.

  Green.flac, Green.txt, Black.flac, Black.txt - Black And Green Guitars
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars
    A green Gretsch Anniversary and a black Hofner Club, recorded and photographed by Brian Wood.
    Copyright 2022 Karoryfer Lecolds.

Changes made for Fret Not: the 24-bit samples were rounded to 16 bits (TPDF dither) and joined into one FLAC per
guitar (Shinyguitar's pickup and microphone recordings as the left and right channels of one file; the Black And
Green packs also hold a one-cycle sine table that stands in for the original's *sine oscillator); the SFZ
mappings were compiled into the .txt files for the plugin's port of the sfizz engine. The sound is
otherwise unchanged.

Adapted from "Divina Madre" by Paulo de Loyola (https://www.deviantart.com/deloyola).
Changes: cropped (crown and face), converted to a single-colour (scarlet) translucent watermark
and faded at the sides and bottom. The eye glow images are made from the
painted eye shapes (darkness mapped to a red glow); the crown glow images from the gold of
the painted halo; the plate and halo images split the watermark into the halo and the rest of the
picture so the halo can turn behind her head.

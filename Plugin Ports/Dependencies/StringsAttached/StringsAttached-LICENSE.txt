Strings Attached - sample data licence
======================================

StringsAttached.flac (Dependencies/StringsAttached) holds the 157 mono samples of Conner Sawchuk's "David's CP70"
(Pianobook, https://www.pianobook.co.uk/library/davids-cp70/), 16-bit 44.1 kHz, joined into one FLAC file without
other changes. StringsAttached.txt holds the region data (key and velocity zones, round robins, sample start
points, levels, tuning, velocity response and amplitude envelope) read from his Kontakt instrument "David's
CP70.nki". Both are played by a JSFX port of the sfizz SFZ engine.

Samples and mapping: Conner Sawchuk. Used and redistributed here with the author's permission. They are not
covered by the JSFX licence, and permission to use them elsewhere must come from the author.

The JSFX code (StringsAttached.jsfx) is released separately under the GPLv3+ licence.

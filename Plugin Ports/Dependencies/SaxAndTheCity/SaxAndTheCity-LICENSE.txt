Sax and the City - sample data licence
======================================

The samples and the instrument mapping in this folder (Soprano.flac, Alto.flac, Tenor.flac, Baritone.flac and
SaxAndTheCity.txt) come from MTG Solo Saxophones:

  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/MTG.SoloSax

  A complete set of Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Baritone solo saxophones built from the MTG free sample packs
  published on freesound.org by the Music Technology Group (MTG), Universitat Pompeu Fabra:
    https://freesound.org/people/MTG/packs/20251/
    https://freesound.org/people/MTG/packs/20239/
    https://freesound.org/people/MTG/packs/20247/
    https://freesound.org/people/MTG/packs/20253/
  Samples trimmed, looped, converted to FLAC and programmed into SFZ by kinwie.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for Sax and the City: the 24-bit samples were rounded to 16 bits (TPDF dither) and joined into one
FLAC per saxophone; the SFZ mapping was compiled into SaxAndTheCity.txt for the plugin's port of the sfizz engine.
The sound is otherwise unchanged.

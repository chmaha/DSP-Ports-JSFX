Evangelina Symphonica Espressivo Brass Ensemble sample and table files
(Dependencies/EvangelinaSymphonica/EspressivoBrass*)
==============================================================================

These files are derived from Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra v4 (https://github.com/peastman/sso), originally created
by Mattias Westlund in 2011 and now developed by Peter Eastman and contributors. The FLAC files hold the section
samples this ensemble plays, joined into one file per section; samples recorded at 24 bits were rounded to 16 bits.
The .txt file is the instrument mapping as numeric tables. The sound of the samples is otherwise unchanged.

The articulation set (sustains and looped sustains with expression or velocity control) follows ideas
from S. Christian Collins' Sonatina v4 ensemble programming and his SCC Expressive Strings
(https://www.schristiancollins.com). The mapping itself was written for this plugin.

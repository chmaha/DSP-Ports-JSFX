A quick start for each plugin, written around what you see and click on screen. Most plugins have a custom graphical interface; the names below are the labels shown in it.

Knobs. On the effects with round knobs, drag a knob up or down, use the mouse wheel to nudge it, and double-click to reset it to its default. Dr. Nine, Synthy McSynthface, the Oscilloscope and the VU Meter also offer fine adjustment (Ctrl or Shift while dragging). Everything is automatable from REAPER too.

Instruments

Captain's Kit, Led Head, Goldibops, Busk Stop

Sampled kits (Black Pearl, Red Zeppelin, Blonde Bop and Buskman's Holiday). Click a piece of the kit photo to hear it, and use the mouse wheel over the photo to change the audition velocity. The pieces light up when played from MIDI on the General MIDI drum notes (36–61; Goldibops and Busk Stop start at 35). Hi-hats and cymbals choke. The controls are Level, MIDI Channel and Display (text, highlight or both). Goldibops has a Sticks / Hot Rods switch. Captain's Kit, Led Head and Goldibops can also output separate channels (kick, snare, hi-hat, tom, floor tom, overheads, percussion); Busk Stop is stereo only.

Chamber Organ

Click the stops (Principal 8', Flute 8', Principal 4', Flute 4', Octave 2') to switch them on and off. Choose a Temperament from the list of 80 historical tunings and set the concert pitch. Retune applies the new tuning. Reverb Settings opens delay, time, width and amount. There is also a Volume control with an output meter and a MIDI Channel choice.

Dr. Nine

Three pages: KIT, PATTERN and FX / MASTER.

  • KIT: knobs for each voice (kick, snare, three toms, rim shot, clap, hi-hats, ride, crash): tune, decay, drive, a distortion type (Diode, Clip, SAT, BFZ, PDIST, Fold, Crush), pan, level, and reverb and delay sends.
  • PATTERN: a 16-pattern step sequencer. The big SEQUENCER ON/OFF button (visible on every page) arms the sequencer. When it is ON (green), the selected pattern plays and loops in sync with REAPER's transport whenever the project is playing or recording, and the button reads RUNNING with a blinking dot on the beat. When it is OFF (red), the sequencer stays silent and Dr. Nine only responds to incoming MIDI. Pattern changes made while it runs take effect when the pattern next loops. It has PLAY/STOP (a preview that works only while REAPER's transport is stopped, with an optional LOOP), SHUFFLE, ACCENT, COPY / PASTE / CLEAR (Shift-click copies a single row or all patterns) and MIDI OUT to send the pattern to other instruments.
  • FX / MASTER: reverb, delay, master distortion and the glue compressor.

Play it from MIDI using the NOTE MAP (General MIDI drum notes or chromatic from C1). Drag knobs, use Ctrl or Shift for fine control, the wheel to nudge, and double-click to reset.

Evangelina Symphonica Espressivo String Ensemble

A sampled string ensemble. The INSTRUMENT page has the CONTROLS choice (Performance, with dynamics on the mod wheel, or Notation, with dynamics from velocity) and the ARTICULATION list: legato (expression or velocity), staccato, tremolo (expression or velocity), legato / tremolo crossfaded with the soft pedal (CC67) and pizzicato. Click an articulation, or play its key switch, to select it. The big dial in the middle shows the live expression value; the Expression controller menu picks whether it follows the mod wheel, breath, foot or CC11.

Along the bottom, Level and Pan set the part, MIDI channel picks Omni or one channel, the Sustain and Sostenuto lamps show the pedals, and the status text shows voices in use or loading progress. Output (top right) sets the master level. Click SETTINGS at the top left for Concert pitch (A4), Temperament and Presets, Polyphony, Sample quality and Pitch bend range. The clickable keyboard plays the instrument. The first load reads the sample file, so allow a moment.

JamSynth (subtractive synth)

One screen, laid out as OSC 1, OSC 2, FILTER, FILTER ENVELOPE, AMP ENVELOPE, LFO, reverb, distortion and KEYBOARD (Poly, Mono or Legato, with glide). Pick a bank and preset from the browser. STORE saves your sound into the 128-slot user bank, where you can also INIT and rename slots. JamSynth Presets is the compact browser-only version (level, MIDI channel, polyphony and bend range).

Jexed (DX7-style FM synth)

Two pages: PLAY and EDIT. On PLAY, the cartridge shows 32 voice cards. Click a card to select it and edit it, or click the cartridge name to switch to another one (Dexed_01 and SynprezFM_01–32 are included). STORE saves the current voice into a user cartridge and INIT resets it. On EDIT you shape each operator (envelope, level, key scaling, frequency, detune), the ALGORITHM and feedback, the LFO & GLOBAL settings and the CONTROLLERS (mod wheel, foot, breath, aftertouch routing to pitch, amp and EG bias). The PLAY page also has output level, MIDI channel, polyphony, cutoff and resonance.

Jexed Presets is the compact version: a cartridge browser plus output level, MIDI channel and polyphony, with no editor.

Kaminari (taiko drums)

Fifteen drums and hits on key pairs from C2 to A#4. Each pair is a white key and the black key above it, so you can roll between them. How long you hold a key sets how long the drum rings (use the sustain pedal for full decays). Click a drum in the DRUMS list to hear it. The CONTROLS panel chooses how dynamics work: Performance (a controller such as the mod wheel, breath, foot or expression, plus velocity) or Notation (velocity only). SETTINGS holds ENGINE options (sample quality and polyphony) and tuning (concert pitch and temperament).

Lord of the Rinks (tonewheel organ)

Set the drawbars for UPPER, LOWER and PEDALS by dragging them. Panels cover KEYBOARD & LEVELS (MIDI channel per manual, split points, transpose), PERCUSSION & VIBRATO (volume, decay, harmonic, vibrato/chorus type), OVERDRIVE · REVERB, ROTORS (the Leslie, with horn and drum Stop/Slow/Fast, brake and acceleration), MICROPHONES & CABINET and FILTERS, and DETAILS (tonewheel and key-click modelling; changes to the tone generator are applied when you release the knob). STORE saves the panel as a program; type a name and press Enter. MIDI LEARN maps a hardware controller to a control: choose a control, then move the controller.

Major General MIDI

A 16-channel General MIDI synth. The CHANNELS panel sets the instrument, level, pan and reverb/chorus sends for each part (channel 10 is the drum kit). FOLLOWS makes one channel play another's instrument for layering. REVERB and CHORUS offer presets, and CC91 SENDS / CC93 SENDS or WHOLE MIX choose whether they follow the per-channel sends or process the whole mix. Other controls: MASTER volume, TUNING and TEMPERAMENT (drums are never retuned), and GM RESET. Over the A4 tuning field, the wheel changes pitch by 1 Hz.

Scoring Synth and Sound Blasted!

SoundFont synths (GeneralUser GS and HL4MGM) with 16 multitimbral channels. The CHANNELS panel sets the bank and program, level, pan and effect sends for each channel, plus FOLLOWS layering. Click a channel number to choose which channel the on-screen keyboard plays. Global controls: REVERB (engine FDN, Freeverb or Dattorro, with room size, damp, width and level), CHORUS, MASTER volume, polyphony, INTERPOLATION and NOTE CUT. A MIDI MONITOR shows incoming messages (the NOTES button shows or hides notes, CLEAR empties it), and GM RESET sends a reset. Tuning and temperament are as in Major General MIDI; hold Ctrl while wheeling over A4 for 0.1 Hz steps.

Super Color

Click an icon in the grid to choose a sound (fifteen in all). Play it from MIDI between B3 and G5, or click and drag across the on-screen keyboard. LIMIT VOICES switches the original's monophonic/three-voice note handling on or off.

Synthy McSynthface

Four instruments in one. Click a tab to choose Piano, ePiano, JX10 or DX10. A display shows the current program name and MIDI activity; step through programs there. Each tab has its own panels of knobs (Ctrl-drag for fine adjustment). Set the MIDI CHANNEL and POLYPHONY in the boxes at the top, and watch the master volume meter's CLIP light.

Tolerable Tines

An electric piano with an 88-key on-screen keyboard that you can also play from the computer keyboard. A MIDI CHANNEL box filters incoming MIDI and KEY VELOCITY sets the velocity of the on-screen keys. The default sound is Tolerable. If you find it merely tolerable, click the button to switch to the Mystery sound, which changes slightly every day. The plugin also hides six ear-training keyboard games (Name That Note, Higher or Lower?, Interval Spotter, Chord Detective, Simon Plays and a scale game) with Easy to Extreme levels, and a December lights display that plays Jingle Bells when clicked.

Effects

Analog EQ

Five band knobs (10 Hz, 40 Hz, 160 Hz, 640 Hz, 2.5 kHz) cut or boost up to 10 dB. Air adds a high shelf, with a corner set from 2.5 kHz to 40 kHz. Analog, Mastering and Keep Gain are switches, and Trim sets the output level.

Analog Jam

A tape stop and tape start. Automate the Mode parameter (Bypass, Stop or Start) to stop or start the tape; the display shows the state (running, stopping, stopped or starting) and the current speed. SLOWDOWN and SPEEDUP each have a length, curve, start and end. Turn SYNC on to set the lengths in bars (1/32 up to 8) from the host tempo. FADE and XFADE smooth the transitions, AUTO BYP returns to bypass automatically, and FILTER (low, high or band pass, CUTOFF, RESO) colours the tape.

ChronoDupe

Automatic double tracking. VOICES has the PAN, DELAY and PITCH spread; FILTER has the HPF and LPF; LEVEL sets DRY and WET.

Compressor

Knobs: Input Gain, Threshold, Ratio (Lim at maximum), Attack and Release. Hold keeps the gain reduction steady. The graph button switches between Curve (input to output) and History (level and gain reduction scrolling from right to left).

De-Esser

Choose Mode: Standard or Advanced. Knobs: Intensity, Low Limit, High Limit and Max DeEss. Advanced mode adds Smoothing, Sharpness, Depth and Filter. Stereo Link ties both channels together and Monitor: Delta lets you hear what is being removed. A Reduction meter shows the effect.

Delay

Knobs: Time, Feedback, LPF, Dry/Wet and Gain. Click Sync BPM to set the time by note value instead (Whole, Half, Quarter, Eighth or Sixteenth). Invert flips polarity.

Dizzy (rotary speaker)

Horn and drum columns, each with STOP / SLOW / FAST speed, BRAKE, ACCEL, DECEL and level. A link setting makes one rotor follow the other or run independently. The FILTERS section shapes each rotor, and the microphone section sets ANGLE, DISTANCE, and the horn and drum RADIUS. The header switches between ENABLED and BYPASSED.

Dynamic Tilt

A tilt EQ pivoting at Pivot. The detector is either Level or Brightness, each with its own Threshold, Amount or Ratio, Max Tilt or Range, Attack and Release. In Brightness mode, Learn averages the input's brightness to set the threshold for you, and Two-Sided lets the tilt go in either direction. Auto Gain keeps the loudness steady and Output trims the result.

EQ

Click a node on the graph to select it, then adjust its frequency, gain and bandwidth (or resonance for the high and low pass) with the controls underneath. Nodes: highpass, low shelf, bands 1–4, high shelf and lowpass. The mouse wheel over a node changes its bandwidth. Buttons set the enable state, Chn (all, left or right), Pos (spectrum before or after the EQ), Spectro mode, Spd (speed), Res (resolution) and the graph Range.

Final Touch

Three knobs: Warm, Space and Punch. Header buttons: X2 (doubles the effect), Delta (hear only what is being added) and Bypass.

Gate

Knobs: Threshold, Attack, Release, Range (all the way down is Mute) and Makeup. Header buttons: Mode: Gate / Duck and Sidechain: On / Off.

Inflator

Knobs: Input, Effect, Curve and Output. Buttons: Effect In, Band Split and Clip 0 dB. Peak and RMS readouts show the level.

Intelligent Spectral Compressor

A compressor working on every frequency band. The Easy view offers Tame (downward ratio), Recover (upward ratio), Tilt (slope), Mix and Output. Advanced exposes everything: Threshold, Center, Slope, Curve, Mode (Pink Noise, SC Match or SC Comp), SC Link, Attack, Release, Window, Overlap, and Down / Up offset, ratio, rolloff and knee. Auto Threshold and Auto Balance help set levels.

JuKnow Chorus

Click I, I+II or II for the chorus mode, then set TONE and MIX.

Limiter

Knobs: Input Gain, Threshold and Release, with a True Peak button for inter-sample peak limiting. The meter shows Gain Reduction.

Lumina Channel Strip

A full channel strip. From input to output: INPUT (gain, PHASE INV), FILTER (high and low pass with FREQ and RES), GATE, an analog EQ with presets (Flat, Acoustic, Bass, Classical, Electronic, Pop, Rock, Vocal), COMPRESSOR (threshold, ratio, attack, release, HOLD, SC HPF, PARALLEL), SATURATION (the five consoles, DRIVE, MIX), LIMITER and TRUE PEAK metering. Each section can be switched on and off, and PRE EQ / POST EQ sets where the EQ sits relative to the compressor.

Maximizer

Three knobs: Release, Threshold and Ceiling. The display shows gain reduction and output level.

Multiband Compressor

Three bands: LOW, MID and HIGH. Click a band to edit it (the header shows which band is being edited), then set its Threshold, Ratio, Knee, Attack, Release and Makeup. Solo auditions a band. Xover 1 and Xover 2 set the split frequencies, Master Trim sets the output level, and Detect: Avg / Max picks the detector.

Reverb

Knobs: DELAY (pre-delay), XOVER, BASS, RT MID (decay time), DAMP, TONE (two-band EQ), MOD AMT and DRY/WET. MODEL chooses ZR1, MK VII or LEXIS.

Saturation

Choose a Console (Burnley, Maryland, Oxford, Tokyo or Washington), then set Drive and Output.

MIDI

Spaghetti Junction

A rack for the 34 x42 MIDI filters. Build a patch by connecting MIDI IN to MIDI OUT through up to eight filter blocks.

  • Add a filter: right-click the canvas or use ADD FILTER. It goes after the selected block, or at the end of the chain if nothing is selected (hold Shift to leave it unpatched).
  • Connect: drag between an output jack (right side) and an input jack (left side), in either direction.
  • Disconnect: pull a cable well away from its path and release.
  • Edit: drag blocks to arrange them and click one to edit it. Right-click cables, jacks or blocks to disconnect, bypass, remove or reset. Delete removes the selected block.
  • One output can feed several inputs, and an input merges every cable plugged into it, so you can build chains, splits and layers.
  • CABLE SLACK sets how loosely the cables hang. Held notes are released whenever the patch changes.

Requires REAPER 7 or later.

Metering and analysis

None of these change the sound.

Bit Meter

Shows which bits are in use. Freeze holds the display, Integrate averages over time, Chn picks All, L or R, and Reset clears it. It also reports the smallest and largest sample and flags denormals, infinities and NaNs.

K-Meter

Pick the K-12, K-14 or K-20 scale (or any K value between 12 and 23). Peak and RMS are shown, and the dBFS button switches the readout to dBFS.

Loudness Meter (EBU R128)

A radar display plus numeric readouts for momentary, short-term and INTEGRATED loudness, RANGE (LRA), MAX and TRUE PEAK. Choose HISTORY or HISTOGRAM for the radar, Momentary or Short as its source, the History Length, and LUFS or LU units. RESET clears the measurement. True peak is only computed if you switch Compute True Peak on.

Multiband Correlation Meter

Stereo correlation per frequency band. Knobs: Bands, Avg Time and Band Q.

Needle Meter

Choose a style: VU, DIN PPM, EBU PPM, BBC PPM, Nordic PPM or BBC M/S. Ref Level sets which dBFS level is the reference, and the TEST mark shows the alignment level.

Oscilloscope

Click the scope to open or close its settings panel. Settings: XY mode, Time, Scale, Hold, Sync, Grid and the colour (hue, saturation, value). Shift-drag for fine adjustment, double-click to reset.

Phase/Frequency Wheel

Shows stereo phase against frequency. Knobs: FFT size, N/Oct (bands per octave), Persist, Gain (or Auto-Gain) and Cutoff.

Spectrum Analyzer

Buttons: FFT Size, Window (Hann, Blackman-Harris or Flat Top), Pink 1/f Spectrum (flattens pink noise) and Peak Hold.

Stereo Phase Scope

A goniometer. Open Settings for Gain (with Autogain), Attack, Decay, Target zoom, RMS/Peak, Lines or points with Line Width and Point Size, Oversample, Persistence and Limit FPS.

Stereo/Frequency Scope

Stereo image by frequency. Knobs: FFT size, N/Oct and Persist.

Test Signal Generator

Pick a signal: 1 kHz sine or square, uniform or Gaussian white noise, pink or brown noise, 100 Hz impulses, sawtooth or triangle, a single impulse, slow impulses (1 Hz and 0.2 Hz), and log or linear sine sweeps. Set the Signal Level (EBU reference is -18 dBFS and US/AU is -20 dBFS). Double-click to reset it, or right-click to type an exact value. Lower your monitoring before you start.

True Peak

A true-peak (dBTP) meter. Reset clears the held peak, and a warning appears when the signal goes over 0 dBTP.

Tuner

Choose Auto, Fixed Note or Fixed Freq, and set the reference pitch under Tuning (A4). A strobe display shows the note and cents offset. Advanced reveals the detection thresholds (RMS, PostFilt, FFT, Override, Fundmntl, Octave, Overtone) and the input source (Mono Sum, Left or Right).

VU Meter

Click the meter to open or close its settings panel. Settings: Nominal level, Rise and Decay speed, and Mono or Stereo. Shift-drag for fine adjustment, double-click to reset.
